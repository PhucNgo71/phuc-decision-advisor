# UI Direction — P Decision Advisor

## Core visual metaphor
The product should feel like a living multi-dimensional brain rather than a conventional CRM dashboard. The homepage uses many connected dots/nodes around a central "P AI Brain" to communicate that every recommendation is built from multiple interacting dimensions.

## Brain nodes
- Product Brain
- Customer Brain
- Commercial Brain
- Relationship Brain
- Competition Brain
- Execution Brain
- Sustainability Brain
- Project Brain
- Learning Brain

Nodes are context-sensitive. They are not fixed scores and should surface conflicts, missing information, trade-offs and what could change the advice.

## Mascot
Use the friendly sea-lion mascot as the recognizable icon/guide for P. It should make the advisor approachable without making the business product feel childish.

## Product principle
AI advises. P decides.
