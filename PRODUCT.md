# Product Definition — Phuc Decision Advisor

A web-based, multi-dimensional strategic decision advisor for workplace projects. It does not make final decisions for the user. It analyzes context, exposes trade-offs, challenges assumptions, and recommends a direction with confidence and conditions that could change the advice.

## Core principle

AI remembers. AI calculates. AI compares. AI challenges. AI advises. Human decides.

## v0.1 user flow

1. Create an opportunity.
2. Define what winning means for this project.
3. Capture customer, relationship, competition, product, commercial and execution context.
4. Generate 2–3 viable strategic scenarios.
5. Compare trade-offs, risks and unknowns.
6. Recommend a direction and show what could change the recommendation.
7. Record the human decision and later the outcome.
8. Learn reusable patterns from decisions and corrections.

## Primary screens

- Advisor Chat
- Opportunity Strategy
- Product Brain
- Decision Memory
